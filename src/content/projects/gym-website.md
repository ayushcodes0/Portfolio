## Overview

A frontend project exploring how a local business site can feel premium: bold typography, strong imagery and a layout that works just as well on a phone as on a desktop.

## Highlights

- Fully responsive layout across mobile, tablet and desktop.
- A sleek, modern visual style aimed at gym owners who want their business to look premium online.
- A smooth browsing experience from the first screen to the call to action.
