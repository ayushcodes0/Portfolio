## Overview

Preparing for JEE means hunting for the same materials again and again: previous-year papers, notes, formula sheets and practice sets scattered across many different sites and links. JEEArchive.in collects all of it in one clean, searchable place.

## Impact

It grew into my most-used product: **1.2M+ students** have used it, and it serves **8,000+ daily active users**.

## What I focused on

- **Everything in one place.** Study materials and resources gathered and organized so students reach the right one quickly.
- **Speed.** Pages are kept light and fast, so the site stays usable on phones and slow connections.
- **Trust.** A clean, consistent experience that students come back to every day.

## What came next

The feedback from this audience led directly to [JEEArchive](/work/jeearchive-mock-tests), a practice and mock-test platform built on the same foundation.
