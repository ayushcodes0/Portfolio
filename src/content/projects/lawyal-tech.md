## Overview

Lawyal Tech builds Contract Coder, a contract lifecycle platform for legal teams. As a frontend developer intern, I worked on the product experience, with the goal of helping new users understand a powerful tool quickly.

## What I built

- **Interactive onboarding walkthrough.** A step-by-step guided tour in React.js that walks new users through the platform's core workflows, boosting the new-user completion rate by **45%** and reducing first-session drop-off.
- **15+ reusable components.** Redux-connected components that cut design-to-implementation time by **15%** across multiple feature modules.
- **Redux state management** to keep tour progress and UI state predictable across the app.
- **Responsive, intuitive UI** that matches the rest of the platform on every screen size.

## Collaboration

I collaborated with the design and backend teams to make sure the tour felt seamless and fit naturally into the existing product.
