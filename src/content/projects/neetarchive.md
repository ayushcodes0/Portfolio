## Overview

NEET Archive brings the [JEEArchive.in](/work/jeearchive-materials) model to medical aspirants. NEET students face the same problem JEE students do: books, notes and practice material scattered across the internet. NEET Archive puts it all in one organized place.

## Impact

Since launch, NEET Archive has received **140,000+ unique visits** and serves **1,500+ daily active users**.

## What's on it

- **Subject-wise libraries.** Physics, Chemistry and Biology books, each with its own resource page.
- **Coaching materials and test series** for structured practice.
- **Handwritten notes, short notes and NCERT books** for quick revision.
- **Class 9 and 10 material** for students who start early.
- **A Discord community** where students get help and discuss preparation.

## Built on what worked

NEET Archive follows the same approach as JEEArchive.in: a clear category structure and a dedicated page for every resource. The two sites link to each other, so students preparing for both exams can move between them easily.
